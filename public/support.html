Support

What is this?

7MW is a free, no-frills seven-minute workout timer. It calls out each exercise, counts you down, and keeps your screen awake while you move. There are no menus to learn — just one START button.

How do I use it?

Open the app, press START, and follow the voice cues and the on-screen demonstration. Tap PAUSE any time. Side plank runs fifteen seconds per side, with a "switch sides" cue at the halfway mark.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

The short version

7MW collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of any kind. Everything happens on your device, and nothing you do ever leaves it.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. We do not use cookies, device identifiers, or location data. The app has no servers and no backend, so there is nowhere for your information to go.

Data stored on your device

The app's voice cues and animations are bundled into the app itself. The app does not write any personal data to your device. There is nothing to export and nothing to delete, because nothing is stored about you.

Third parties

7MW shares no data with third parties because it has no data to share. The app integrates no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social-media services.

Children's privacy

7MW is safe for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
